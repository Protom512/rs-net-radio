## 2025-05-14 - [Color-Symbol Pairing for Accessibility]
**Learning:** Purely color-based status indicators can be inaccessible to users with color vision deficiencies. Combining ANSI colors with descriptive symbols (emojis like ✅, ❌, ⚠️) ensures that status information is perceivable regardless of color vision.
**Action:** Always pair status-indicating colors with unique symbols or text labels to maintain accessibility in CLI interfaces.
